# Milena Strahova — Game Design Portfolio

**Game Design • Unreal Engine 5 • Gameplay Systems • Prototyping • C++ / Blueprints**

I am a Game Design student in Riga with hands-on experience building playable Unreal Engine projects from concept to packaged prototype. My focus is on gameplay loops, encounter design, player feedback, progression, stealth/combat systems and practical iteration through playtesting.
