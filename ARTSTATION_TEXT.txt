ASHEN KEEP: BLOOD HUNT

Ashen Keep: Blood Hunt is a compact dark-fantasy Unreal Engine 5 vertical
slice built primarily in C++.

Play as an ancient vampire reclaiming her castle from monster hunters before
they complete a purge ritual. The project includes server-authoritative melee
combat, Mist Step, Blood Burst, vampiric recovery, lock-on targeting, hunter
AI, a captain encounter, replicated objectives, a custom HUD, main menu,
victory screen, original music and original sound effects.

TECHNICAL FOCUS
• Unreal Engine 5 C++ and Blueprint integration
• Component-based gameplay architecture
• RPCs, actor replication and multiplayer validation
• AI vision cone, line of sight, pursuit and combat
• Automated tests for attributes and replication
• CPU, GPU, memory and network profiling
• Windows Shipping build

MY RESPONSIBILITIES
Gameplay programming, systems design, Blueprint integration, UI/UX,
level assembly, lighting, character setup, debugging, profiling, testing,
audio creation and final presentation.
